Italian spell-check word list for PureQuill Writer
=================================================

it.dic is a flat list of Italian word forms extracted from the surface
forms (column 1) of the Morph-it! morphological lexicon, version 0.4.8.
Elision fragments (l, d, c, un, dell, all, nell, ...) and a small list of
common present-day/technical terms were added on top.

it.aff (the affix file) contains no Morph-it! data — only a character
set declaration, a suggestion-ordering hint and a few replacement rules —
and is released into the public domain.
